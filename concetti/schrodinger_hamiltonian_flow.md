# Schrödinger Equation as Hamiltonian Flow

**Stated in tesi**: §3.3
**Links**: [[stone_theorem]], [[kahler_geometry]], [[noether_theorem_quantum]], [[capitoli/cap3]]

## Contenuto

Il risultato-chiave del Cap. 3, metà "top-down" della domanda centrale della tesi: l'equazione di Schrödinger *è* l'equazione del flusso Hamiltoniano di $f_H$ su $(\mathbb{P}\mathcal{H},\omega)$. Segue immediatamente dalla discesa di Stone (già in [[stone_theorem]]) — qui va solo reso esplicito che le due equazioni coincidono, non solo che i flussi coincidono.

---

## Testo LaTeX pronto per la tesi (§3.3)

```latex
\begin{theorem}[Schrödinger equation as Hamiltonian flow]\label{thm:schrodinger-hamiltonian}
Let $\hat H$ be self-adjoint on $\mathcal{H}$ and
$f_H([\psi])=\dfrac{\langle\psi|\hat H|\psi\rangle}{\langle\psi|\psi\rangle}$. A curve
$t\mapsto[\psi(t)]$ solves the projected Schrödinger equation
\[
i\hbar\,\dot\psi(t) = \hat H\,\psi(t)
\]
if and only if $t\mapsto[\psi(t)]$ is an integral curve of the Hamiltonian vector
field $X_{f_H}$ on $(\mathbb{P}\mathcal{H},\omega)$ of
Proposition~\ref{prop:PH-kahler}.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof (sketch)]
By Proposition~\ref{prop:stone-descent}, $[\psi(t)]=\Phi_t([\psi(0)])$ with $\Phi_t$
the descended Stone flow, which is by definition the Hamiltonian flow of $f_H$.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
This is the top-down half of the thesis's central claim: Hamiltonian structure is
not added to quantum mechanics — it is already present in the unitary dynamics
generated by Stone's theorem, once state space is correctly identified as
$\mathbb{P}\mathcal{H}$ rather than $\mathcal{H}$.
\end{remark}
```
