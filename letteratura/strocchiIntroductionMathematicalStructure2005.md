# Strocchi (2008)

**Full reference**: F. Strocchi, *An Introduction to the Mathematical Structure of Quantum Mechanics*, World Scientific, 2nd ed. (2008).

**Used in thesis**: §1.5 (C*-algebra definitions), §2.1 (operational approach), §2.2 (GNS), §2.3 (Wintner)

---

## Key results used

### Wintner–Wielandt theorem

In any unital Banach algebra, AB − BA = I is impossible. Proof by induction (no trace, no finite-dimension assumption). See Ch. 1 of Strocchi.

### Operational approach (Ch. 1)

Starting from preparations and measurements as primitive data, derives the C*-algebra structure without postulating a Hilbert space. States as positive normalised functionals.

### GNS construction

Standard construction; uniqueness via explicit unitary isomorphism.

---

## Pedagogical notes

Strocchi's approach is particularly clean for the **parallel between CM and QM**: both are C*-algebras, one commutative and one not. The operational starting point (no ℋ) is exactly what Ch. 2 needs.
